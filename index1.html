Mission

Tech Connect exists to break down silos in tech by creating a space where members:

1. Network with intent

Meet people before you need a job, client, or collaborator.

2. Learn in public

Share projects, get feedback, and grow skills in web development, software engineering, data, design, web3, and more.

3. Create opportunities

Surface jobs, freelance gigs, and project collabs so members can turn skills into income and impact.

Vision

To be the most connected and practical tech community where people from different skills — devs, designers, analysts, founders — meet, learn, and build things that matter together.
